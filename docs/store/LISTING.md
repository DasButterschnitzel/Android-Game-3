## English

**App name (max 30):** Nachhall – Echoes of the Night

**Short description (max 80):** Defend the lantern – every attempt becomes an echo that fights with you.

**Full description:**
A paper lantern hangs above a sleeping town. Ink ghosts are coming.
You have eight seconds – and you are not alone: every run becomes an echo that replays exactly what you did.
Plan with your past selves, hit twins at the same moment and crack armored ghosts with two lights.

• Short nights of one to three minutes – made for quick breaks
• Play with one finger; a second finger lights a second light
• Bank now or risk one more light? Fewer lights, more points
• Every light sings its own note: your echoes sound like a canon
• Daily night: the same night for everyone, new every day
• Full-moon mode and constellations to unlock (cosmetic only)
• Fully offline, no account, no purchases, no energy timers
